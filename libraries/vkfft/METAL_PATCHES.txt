include/vkFFT.h is VkFFT 1.2.33 with fixes to its Metal backend (VKFFT_BACKEND 5), which the
Metal platform uses.  They belong upstream (https://github.com/DTolm/VkFFT).

- NS::Error pointers passed to newLibrary() and newComputePipelineState() are initialized to
  NULL, and are checked for NULL before their descriptions are printed.
- MTL::CompileOptions objects are initialized with init(), not only allocated.
- A shader compile has failed when newLibrary() returns no library.  A non-null error alone
  does not mean failure, since compiler warnings set it too.
- Objects that metal-cpp returns autoreleased (command buffers and encoders from
  commandBuffer() and computeCommandEncoder()/blitCommandEncoder(), and strings from
  NS::String::string()) are no longer released, which over-released them and crashed when the
  autorelease pool drained.
